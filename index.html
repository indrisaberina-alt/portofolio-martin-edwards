More than an idol.

03 — CAREER JOURNEY

The road to becoming an idol.

Every achievement starts with a small step. Here is Martin's journey through music.

2017

Early Musical Creativity

Martin reportedly released an original musical composition through Dream Darak Saturday Culture School, showing an early interest in music creation.

2018

Winter Olympics Experience

He participated in the children's choir at the PyeongChang Winter Olympics as part of the Multicultural Family Rainbow Choir. He was also reported to have carried Iceland's flag.

2019–2020

BIGHIT MUSIC Audition

Encouraged by his older sister, Martin auditioned for BIGHIT MUSIC. He performed Justin Bieber's "Baby" and successfully passed while in fifth grade.

2020–2023

Trainee Years

He trained in performance and music while learning rap writing, freestyle, vocal recording, and music production using Cubase. He also collaborated creatively with other trainees.

2021

Freestyle Dance Battle

Martin appeared in Showdown Vol.6 Freestyle Dance Battle, where he faced Lee Sang Won.

2023

Trainee Interview

He appeared in publicly shared BIGHIT trainee interview content alongside Sung Hyun.

2024

Songwriting & Production Credits

Before his group debut, Martin contributed to songs by ILLIT, TXT, and LE SSERAFIM. His work included Magnetic, Deja Vu, Miracle, and Pierrot.

2025

Official Debut with CORTIS

Martin officially debuted as a member of CORTIS on August 18, 2025, under BIGHIT MUSIC. He became the group's leader.

2026

Growing as a Creative Artist

Martin continued participating in CORTIS music releases and creative projects, including the group's second EP, GREENGREEN.
